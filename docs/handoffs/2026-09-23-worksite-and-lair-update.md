Minecraft MOBA — Worksite Cadence + Economy Reconciliation
Date: 2026-09-23

Please inspect the current repository and reconcile the Worksite system to the design decisions below. Do not invent missing mechanics or balance values. Preserve newer doctrine where it does not conflict with this handoff, and report any genuine conflicts rather than silently resolving them.

This handoff supersedes the older FOUR-WORKSITE economic progression:
    Copper → Iron → Diamond → Ancient Debris
and:
    Blast Furnace/Smoker → TBD intermediate Factory → Enchanting → Smithing

The current match cadence has THREE Worksite nights, alternating with the three Giant Monster Lair nights:

    Worksite I
    → Giant
    → Worksite II
    → Ghast
    → Worksite III
    → Ender Dragon

The Worksite economy should now be reconciled cleanly to those three Worksite nights.

============================================================
1. WORKSITE SYSTEM IDENTITY — PRESERVE
============================================================

Do not redesign the general Worksite architecture.

Worksites are distributed, finite or capability-specific exceptional economic opportunities. They are distinct from Infrastructure, ordinary world resources, regenerative resources, POIs, and the permanent Giant Monster Lair.

The two established Worksite forms are:

MINING SITE
    enabling avenue: Construction
    payoff avenue: Extraction

INDUSTRIAL FACTORY
    enabling avenue: Logistics
    payoff avenue: Production

The high-level grammar remains:

    enabling avenue
        ↓
    Worksite capitalization
        ↓
    payoff avenue

More specifically:

MINING SITE

A dormant Mining Site may activate with a phase-specific resource opportunity.

Activation communicates/prospects the opportunity, but the exceptional ore manifestation does not physically exist beforehand in a bypassable form.

A qualifying Construct capitalizes the Mining Site.

Successful capitalization causes the phase-specific exceptional resource geography to manifest/become exposed.

Once manifested, these are ordinary physical Minecraft blocks:
- they occupy actual world geography;
- players must reach them;
- players must excavate them;
- players must collect them;
- players must transport/use them normally;
- they persist as world state;
- they can be contested by either team.

The apparatus model is obsolete. Do not restore a special Mining Outpost machine.

The site should not be an ore dispenser, chest, automatic mine, or abstract loot payout.

INDUSTRIAL FACTORY

A dormant Industrial Factory may activate with a phase-specific special workstation.

Activation exposes the workstation, but activation alone does not make its special Factory capability operational.

Logistics is the enabling challenge.

Factory operation should be based on relevant real inputs being available at the Factory, not an invented Factory currency, arbitrary progress bar, generic "power," or mandatory recognized Supply Line.

Ordinary physical delivery, storage, transport, caches, Supply Lines where useful, and other Logistics behavior can contribute naturally.

Production then converts the supplied inputs through the Factory opportunity.

Do not manufacture artificial exclusive ownership. Physical control can be contested.

Preserve the distinction:

    Mining = establish a useful PLACE
    Factory = establish a useful FLOW

2. THREE-WORKSITE MATERIAL / FACILITY PROGRESSION
============================================================

The current three Worksite identities are:

WORKSITE I
Mining Site:
    IRON + COAL

Industrial Factory:
    BLAST FURNACE + SMOKER

Economic role:
    bulk material / processing
    establish the industrial economy

WORKSITE II
Mining Site:
    DIAMOND + LAPIS

Industrial Factory:
    ENCHANTING TABLE

Economic role:
    strategic equipment capital / enhancement

WORKSITE III
Mining Site:
    ANCIENT DEBRIS + DIAMOND

Industrial Factory:
    SMITHING TABLE

Economic role:
    apex material / conversion
    constrained Netherite economy

Compactly:

    I:   Iron + Coal
         ↕
         Blast Furnace + Smoker

    II:  Diamond + Lapis
         ↕
         Enchanting Table

    III: Ancient Debris + Diamond
         ↕
         Smithing Table

This is now the intended three-stage Worksite progression.

============================================================
3. REMOVE THE COPPER WORKSITE TIER
============================================================

Copper no longer needs a Worksite phase.

Copper is sufficiently abundant in the ordinary opening world economy and has the material identity:

    replaceable / field-standard metal

It should not require an exceptional Worksite injection to establish its economy.

This also fits the current Opening Hinterland doctrine: fundamental Minecraft verbs and basic material participation should be possible in the opening without the Hinterland resolving later strategic resource questions.

Therefore remove/supersede older doctrine proposing an initial Copper Mining Site such as:

    ~8–12 Copper + Coal

Do NOT remove Copper from the world economy or change its broader material role merely because its Worksite tier is removed.

The old four-step Mining ladder:

    Copper
    → Iron
    → Diamond
    → Ancient Debris

becomes:

    Iron
    → Diamond
    → Ancient Debris

More precisely:

    Iron + Coal
    → Diamond + Lapis
    → Ancient Debris + Diamond

============================================================
4. REMOVE THE TBD INTERMEDIATE FACTORY TIER
============================================================

The old four-step Factory progression contained:

    Blast Furnace + Smoker
    → TBD intermediate/system Factory
    → Enchanting Table
    → Smithing Table

The TBD intermediate Factory was never sufficiently defined and is no longer needed once the cadence has three Worksite nights.

Remove/supersede that placeholder rather than inventing a workstation to fill it.

The Factory progression is now simply:

    Blast Furnace + Smoker
    → Enchanting Table
    → Smithing Table

Conceptually:

    PROCESSING
    → ENHANCEMENT
    → APEX CONVERSION

Do not invent an intermediate transformation tier.

============================================================
5. WORKSITE I — IRON + COAL / PROCESSING
============================================================

Worksite I is the first exceptional Mining Site opportunity.

It does NOT mean Iron is impossible before Worksite I.

Ordinary Extraction must remain possible.

However, current Opening Hinterland doctrine specifically rejects handing players enough conveniently accessible Iron to shortcut the intended opening by simply equipping a player immediately.

So Worksite I represents the first major CONCENTRATED exceptional Iron opportunity.

Its Mining Site backbone is:

    Iron + Coal

The previous quantitative sensitivity for the Iron tier was approximately:

    ~40–50 physical Iron ore

with earlier discussion allowing smaller phase-appropriate system-resource components.

That quantity is NOT canon balance.

Do not hard-code it as final unless the implementation already requires a fixture, in which case mark it explicitly provisional/calibration-only.

For the reconciled identity, Coal is now the clean secondary material rather than needing to guarantee a broad Redstone/Lapis/Gold pocket.

The paired Factory exposes:

    Blast Furnace
    Smoker

These use recognizable Minecraft processing relationships.

Do not conclude that ordinary Blast Furnaces/Smokers are prohibited unless current canonical Production doctrine explicitly says so.

The Factory context is the exceptional Worksite opportunity; ordinary Minecraft capability should remain broadly universal.

============================================================
6. WORKSITE II — DIAMOND + LAPIS / ENCHANTING
============================================================

Worksite II is the strategic-capital Worksite.

Mining Site:

    Diamond + Lapis

Factory:

    Enchanting Table

This supersedes the recent provisional framing of:

    Diamond + Gold

as the guaranteed Mining Site backbone.

Gold can remain an ordinary-world resource and can appear elsewhere if existing world/resource doctrine permits it. Do not delete Gold from the economy.

The reason for Diamond + Lapis is systemic:

    Diamond = valuable equipment capital
    Lapis = enchantment input
    Enchanting Table = conversion opportunity

Thus the paired Worksite opportunities speak the same economic language without making either one sufficient by itself.

Ordinary-world Diamond and Lapis remain relevant.

Winning Worksite II should NOT transform:

    "we cannot participate in Diamond/enchanting"

into:

    "now we can."

Instead it should transform scarce ordinary participation into a substantially stronger concentration of strategic capital.

Earlier Diamond calibration work found a useful NON-CANON sensitivity:

    ordinary relevant Diamond opportunity: ~14–18 physical ore
    Worksite II Diamond opportunity: ~8–12 physical ore

Central example:

    16 ordinary + 10 Worksite = 26 physical Diamond ore

The economic target behind that sensitivity was roughly:

    normal/no-Yield economy:
        about one major full Diamond investment

    strong Fortune/Yield economy:
        approaching two major Diamond investments

The important doctrine is NOT the exact 8–12 number.

The important doctrine is:

    Diamond remains scarce strategic capital.
    Worksite II concentrates it.
    Extraction specialization magnifies the same physical opportunity through ordinary systemic mechanics such as Fortune/Yield rather than "Worksite loot bonuses."

Earlier Gold quantity fixtures attached to this tier should be treated as superseded where they imply Gold is the guaranteed paired material.

Lapis now fills that paired role.

Earlier Lapis can still have stored option value:
- ordinary Lapis acquired before Worksite II remains useful;
- Worksite II creates an additional concentrated Lapis opportunity at the point enchanting becomes strategically important.

Do not make the Worksite II Lapis manifestation the only source of Lapis.

============================================================
7. WORKSITE III — ANCIENT DEBRIS + DIAMOND / SMITHING
============================================================

Worksite III is the apex-material Worksite.

Mining Site:

    Ancient Debris + Diamond

Factory:

    Smithing Table

This is the constrained Netherite economy.

The Mining Site does NOT dispense Netherite equipment.

The intended chain is closer to:

    Ancient Debris
        +
    existing Gold economy
        +
    existing Diamond equipment/capital
        +
    Logistics convergence
        ↓
    Smithing Factory
        ↓
    limited Netherite upgrades

This is deliberately networked.

A team can:
- control the Mining Site but not the Factory;
- control the Factory but lack Ancient Debris;
- possess Debris but lack appropriate Diamond equipment;
- have the material chain but face transport/control problems.

Late-game complexity should emerge from relationships between Minecraft systems rather than increasingly bespoke Worksite minigames.

Previous NON-CANON calibration sensitivity:

    ordinary Ancient Debris opportunity: ~4–8
    Worksite III: ~8–12 Ancient Debris
    total relevant economy: ~12–20 Ancient Debris

Since Fortune does not multiply Ancient Debris, this gave approximately:

    Worksite alone:
        ~2–3 Netherite Ingots worth of Debris

    ordinary + Worksite:
        ~3–5 Netherite Ingots/upgrades

That is a calibration hypothesis, NOT a finalized quantity.

The intended strategic scale is:

    Netherite = premier late-game capital

not:

    Netherite = universal final equipment tier for the whole team.

Previous apex Diamond sensitivity:

    ~6–10 Diamond

may remain as a provisional calibration hypothesis.

Diamond appears again at Worksite III because Diamond equipment/capital is the substrate for Netherite conversion. It should not displace Ancient Debris as the reason the site matters.

8. MATERIAL-ECONOMY IDENTITY
============================================================

Preserve the broader material progression:

    Stone
        = disposable

    Copper
        = replaceable / field-standard metal

    Iron
        = stable / reliable

    Diamond
        = high-value capital

    Netherite
        = premier late-game capital

Do not interpret the Worksite sequence as exclusive permission to acquire these materials.

The ordinary world economy still matters.

The Worksites provide phase-appropriate exceptional concentrations/opportunities.

This distinction is especially important for Diamond:

    without Worksite II:
        scarce Diamond economy still exists

    with Worksite II:
        team can concentrate substantially more Diamond capital

Likewise, some ordinary Ancient Debris opportunity may exist if that remains compatible with current map/resource doctrine; Worksite III is the major exceptional concentration.

============================================================
9. ESCALATION SHAPE
============================================================

Do NOT model later Mining Sites as simply containing more blocks.

The intended economic escalation is approximately:

    Worksite I:
        higher-volume foundational/system material

    Worksite II:
        lower-volume, higher-value strategic capital

    Worksite III:
        very-low-volume apex conversion material

Conceptually:

    VOLUME
    → CAPITAL
    → APEX CONVERSION

Later Worksites can contain fewer physical ore blocks while being much more economically consequential.

This is desirable.

Likewise Factory progression is:

    PROCESSING
    → ENHANCEMENT
    → CONVERSION

not:

    fast machine
    → faster machine
    → fastest machine

============================================================
10. SPECIALIZATION DOCTRINE
============================================================

Do not implement Worksite specialization as arbitrary class multipliers such as:

    "Extractor gets +50% Worksite loot."

The project doctrine is that specialization should emerge from the player's build, capabilities, Minecraft systems, infrastructure, and strategic role.

For example:

    same 10 physical Diamond ore
        ↓
    different realized yield depending on Fortune/Yield investment

is desirable.

The opportunity is physically shared.

The team's prior specialization determines how efficiently it realizes that opportunity.

Similarly, Production players should become valuable because their builds and surrounding economy make funneling valuable inputs through them rational—not because they selected a generic "+20% quality" Worksite perk.

============================================================

Do not turn Worksites into binary team-owned capture points.

The developed Worksite architecture distinguishes:

1. activation;
2. capitalization;
3. exploitation.

The first team to satisfy the capitalization condition may receive whatever first-capitalization reward current canonical Infrastructure doctrine specifies.

However:
- physical Worksite access remains contestable;
- remaining Mining Site resources remain contestable;
- Factory access/supply remains contestable;
- first capitalization should not create artificial exclusive ownership.

IMPORTANT:
There has been subsequent Infrastructure design work after some of the historical Worksite discussion.

Therefore inspect current Infrastructure doctrine before carrying forward any older statement such as:

    "+1 team Infrastructure Slot on first capture"

Do NOT blindly restore an obsolete Infrastructure Slot model if newer commits superseded it.

Reconcile Worksite first-capitalization rewards to CURRENT Infrastructure doctrine.

The economic/resource/facility decisions in this handoff are authoritative for this reconciliation; old Infrastructure implementation assumptions are not.

11. WORKSITE FIRST CAPITALIZATION / INFRASTRUCTURE SLOTS
============================================================

Retain the current experimental first-capitalization model:

    first qualifying team
        → +1 team Infrastructure Slot

This is currently the newer experimental model being examined.

Do NOT remove it merely because the broader Infrastructure implementation is incomplete.

However, preserve an important caveat:

    the project does NOT yet have a sufficiently realized Infrastructure
    model for the full economic value or final semantics of Infrastructure
    Slots to be considered settled.

Very little actual Infrastructure gameplay currently exists relative to the
design architecture.

Therefore:

- preserve +1 team Infrastructure Slot as the current experimental
  first-capitalization reward;
- preserve it in documentation/config/runtime seams where appropriate;
- do not build large new systems around assumptions about its final value;
- do not treat current slot counts, allocation rules, reassignment rules,
  saturation behavior, or exact infrastructure licensing semantics as
  finalized unless newer canon explicitly resolves them;
- leave clean seams for the Infrastructure model to evolve.

The intended current interpretation is approximately:

    Successfully bringing a new Worksite into the team's economy
        → expands the team's organizational Infrastructure capacity.

The first-capitalization reward is permanent.

It is separate from current physical control of the Worksite.

Thus:

MINING SITE
    first qualifying Construct
        → first capitalization
        → +1 permanent team Infrastructure Slot
        → resource manifestation exposed

    Afterward:
        exposed resources remain physically contestable.

INDUSTRIAL FACTORY
    first qualifying supply relationship
        → first capitalization
        → +1 permanent team Infrastructure Slot
        → Factory becomes operational for that supplied relationship

    Afterward:
        practical access and supply remain contestable.

Do not turn either Worksite into a permanently team-owned capture point.

The experimental Infrastructure reward and the Worksite's physical economic
opportunity are separate state.

============================================================
12. WORKSITE ACTIVATION IS PERMANENT — SUPERSEDES SUNRISE DEACTIVATION
============================================================

Explicitly supersede any older rule, implementation, comment, test, config,
or documentation saying that Worksites deactivate, close, reset, or become
dormant again at sunrise.

SUNSET IS AN ACTIVATION EVENT, NOT AN AVAILABILITY WINDOW.

Once a Worksite is selected and activated:

    it remains active.

Sunrise has NO deactivation effect.

The lifecycle is therefore NOT:

    dormant
        → sunset activation
        → active during night
        → sunrise deactivation

It is:

    dormant
        → selected at scheduled Worksite sunset
        → ACTIVE
        → remains active across all subsequent day/night cycles

For MINING SITES:

    DORMANT
        ↓ scheduled sunset activation
    ACTIVE / PROSPECTIVE
        ↓ qualifying Construct
    CAPITALIZED / MANIFESTED
        ↓ physical extraction
    PARTIALLY DEPLETED
        ↓ continued physical extraction
    DEPLETED

The Mining Site remains an active world location until its finite exceptional
resource opportunity has been depleted.

Sunrise does nothing to it.

If only part of the manifestation is mined before sunrise, the remaining
physical resources remain there and remain contestable during the following
day and every subsequent cycle until depleted.

Do not regenerate or replace the finite Worksite resource manifestation merely
because another day/night cycle occurs.

For INDUSTRIAL FACTORIES:

    DORMANT
        ↓ scheduled sunset activation
    ACTIVE / EXPOSED
        ↓ qualifying Logistics supply
    CAPITALIZED / OPERATIONAL
        ↓ supply/use/contest
    PERSISTENT ACTIVE FACTORY

Factories do not naturally deplete in the same way as Mining Sites.

Once activated, the Factory remains part of the match's industrial geography
for the remainder of the match.

Its current usability may depend on its actual Logistics/input state, but this
is NOT deactivation.

Distinguish:

    Factory is ACTIVE
from:
    Factory is currently SUPPLIED / OPERATIONAL.

An activated Factory with insufficient current inputs is still an activated
Factory. Players can restore the required supply relationship later.

Do not introduce an artificial expiration rule.

This means Worksite geography ACCUMULATES over the match.

For example, after Worksite III has activated, the world may simultaneously
contain:

    depleted or partially depleted Worksite I Mining Sites;
    active Worksite I Blast Furnace/Smoker Factories;
    depleted or partially depleted Worksite II Mining Sites;
    active Worksite II Enchanting Factories;
    active Worksite III Ancient Debris/Diamond Mining Sites;
    active Worksite III Smithing Factories.

Their continued relevance is determined by:
- remaining physical resources;
- current supply;
- geography;
- player-created Infrastructure;
- Routes and Practical Reach;
- strategic demand;
- physical control;

not by whether it is currently nighttime.

This persistence is important because the match should accumulate an economic
history and industrial geography rather than replacing one Worksite phase
with the next.

    Night 1 — Worksite I activates
    Night 2 — Giant
    Night 3 — Worksite II activates
    Night 4 — Ghast
    Night 5 — Worksite III activates
    Night 6 — Ender Dragon

"Night 1 / 3 / 5" identifies WHEN new Worksites activate.

It does NOT identify how long those Worksites remain available.

Therefore by Night 5:

    Worksite I opportunities may still matter;
    Worksite II opportunities may still matter;
    Worksite III is newly added to that accumulated economic geography.

Do not implement Worksite tiers as mutually exclusive global phases.

Later Worksite activation does not deactivate or supersede earlier physical
Worksites.

============================================================
14. RELATION TO GIANT MONSTER LAIR
============================================================

Worksites and the Giant Monster Lair remain distinct opportunity systems.

Worksites:
- distributed;
- economic;
- multiple candidate sites;
- selected/activated over the match;
- Mining + Factory opportunities.

Lair:
- exactly one permanent conspicuous central/competitively-central landmark;
- Giant / Ghast / Ender Dragon occupants on their scheduled nights;
- contested combat opportunity;
- successful monster defeat produces its paired objective siege assault.

Do not spatially cluster Worksites around the Lair merely because their nights alternate.
