# Handoffs

Dated consolidations of design conversation and cross-session instructions.

**These are source material, not canon.** `AGENTS.md` names `classes.md`,
`objectives.md`, `maps.md` and `infrastructure.md` as the canonical design
sources for their domains, and `infrastructure.md` for shared infrastructure.
A handoff may preserve **unincorporated context** — material discussed but
never folded into a canonical document — and that is the reason to keep them
rather than delete them. It is not a reason to treat one as authoritative.

Where a handoff and a canonical source disagree, the canonical source wins
unless an explicit reconciliation decision says otherwise, and that decision
belongs in the reconciliation record.

## The 20–23 September pastes

Eight files were moved here on 4 October 2026 from the repository root,
`docs/` and `specs/`, where they had accumulated as raw pastes under names
like `herdswarmpatchetcpaste2.md`. They are ~7,000 lines and several of their
topics have since reached canon — Worksites, Lairs, herds and swarms, founder
stock, second-layer extraction.

They were **not** audited for what remains unincorporated when they were moved.
That audit is worth doing before any of them is assumed spent.
