# Reconciliation — Skeleton Crew and Lightfooted settled

Date: 2026-09-12

## Source

Class Design Handoff `[WS]`: newly settled classes Skeleton Crew and Lightfooted.

## Promotion

Both classes moved **out of** the unsettled drafts section and into full class
sections. Each now satisfies the project standard: hook and core rule, passive,
A1 with three mutually exclusive branches, A2 with three mutually exclusive
branches, and an ultimate.

- `classes.md` §14 previously held four drafts. It now holds **Waxer and
  Daredevil only**.
- Skeleton Crew is `classes.md` §15 / manuscript §9.13.
- Lightfooted is `classes.md` §16 / manuscript §9.14.
- The §11 roster status gained a "Settled beyond the original four" entry.

## Superseded draft content

**Skeleton Crew.** The draft's Undead Affinity ("largely non-hostile undead"),
banking-and-Arise A1, and the bare "set crew ablaze" A2 are replaced by the
attraction-based passive, Graveyard Shift with Field Work / Hard Hat Zone /
Labored Union, and Burning Out with Fire Drill / Deadline / Water Break. The
ultimate is renamed from "Getting A-Head" to **A-Head of Schedule** and given
its specialization rule.

**Lightfooted.** The draft's Rabbit's Foot passive is replaced by **Animal
Senses** with three species-specific stacking bonuses. Thieving Swipe is now
explicitly **mainhand** disable — the handoff states not to revert to the older
offhand-only concept. A2, its branch tree, and the ultimate were previously
recorded as missing and are now filled.

## Archetypes

Assigned descriptively, not for coverage. Skeleton Crew is **Logistics and
Combat**. Lightfooted is **Exploration primary, Combat secondary**; the handoff
explicitly declines Development for managing animals and Logistics for moving
them.

## Consistency with current infrastructure canon

Skeleton Crew's workforce rule matches the Capacity model already committed:
a Crew Member is either physically active or committed as Supply Line Capacity,
never both, and its physical cargo Capacity and committed Capacity are the same
underlying quantity. Flow Weight terminology is not reintroduced. The handoff's
restatement that no universal Summoning Capacity is used agrees with the
existing record.

## Retained [OPEN]

Detection radius, eligible undead, Crew generation values, Crew Limit; armour
material, timeout and friendly damage for Hard Hat Zone; Deadline threshold
(~3s conceptually); what counts as successfully extinguishing for Water Break;
Horseman size, stats, duration and specialization strength; Animal Senses
percentages, radii and caps; wolf scaling and cooldown behaviour; Thieving
Swipe duration, scaling and cap; snow-family and leaf and dirt-family block
tags; base Bound count; and all Rabbit's Lucky Foot geometry and damage values.

Per the handoff, [OPEN] here means numbers and implementation only, and is not
licence to reopen decided mechanics.
