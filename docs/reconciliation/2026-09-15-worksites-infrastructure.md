## Decisions

- Exactly **Mining Site** and **Industrial Factory**. Retire archetype-specific Worksite categories, Mining Outpost, Industrial Enchanter and provisional Forge names for this system.
- **Apparatus is entirely retired**, including its Core/multiblock/prospecting gate. Old Silo/Barrier/fuel-bank and enchanting-only contracts are superseded.
- Surplus dormant pool; selected eligible sites receive activation-phase packages at 6/18/30/42m. No guaranteed individual activation or permanent site tier.
- **Dormant -> Activated -> Capitalized -> Exploited** separates world selection from player work.
- Mining activation communicates prospective composition without special ore exposure. A Construct-compliant building capitalizes the site and exposes finite physical ore for contested Extraction.
- Factory activation exposes workstations; qualifying Logistics supplies/powers/enables productive use. Packages: 6m Blast Furnace + Smoker; 18m **TBD / OPEN**; 30m Enchanting Table; 42m Smithing Table.
- First qualifying capitalization of **every** activated site grants its first team **+1 Infrastructure Slot**. The reward persists through enemy use/loss of control: **First Capture != Exclusive Control**.
- Lv6 grants each member one Infrastructure Slot. Normal class progression grows player capacity; Worksite capture separately grows team capacity. Construct, Route, Supply Line, Development Zone and Working Mob Slayer are eligible concepts. Mob Slayer specifics, later slot cadence and team-slot allocation remain OPEN.
- Mining is finite/depletion oriented; Factory is throughput/input/access oriented. Neither Exploration nor Combat gets its own Worksite.
- Acceleration includes capitalization, exploitation, downstream Work Stock and persistent slot capacity; no flat slot XP. Preserve UAU, Work Stock, Practical Reach and **WP_X = A(O) + qH** with normalized Harvest Units and Fortune increasing qualifying harvest. No sensitivity values are promoted to canon.

## Boundaries and open questions

The phase verbs Establish, Specialize, Capitalize and Resolve are retained. Exact mining packages, communication UI, Factory supply/power thresholds, selection counts/fairness, Mob Slayer rules, team-slot allocation and later slot cadence are OPEN. Existing sunrise closure is Working; it does not erase exposed ore or earned slots. Closure details and reuse are OPEN. Qualifying Logistics does not invent a mandatory recognized Supply Line check.

Historical reconciliation records and browser pages now carry visible supersession notices. The old Word export is moved to `docs/history/` with an explicit superseded filename; the wiki ZIP remains an explicitly historical snapshot.
