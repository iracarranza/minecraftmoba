# Reconciliation — class kit handoffs

Date: 2026-09-13 (two handoffs, same day)

## Source

**[K7]** Seven-class kit handoff: Mole, Gardener, Kitfighter, Golem Master,
Skeleton Crew, Lightfooted, Daredevil. Each entry supplies archetypes, a hook,
a passive, two actives with three branches each, and an ultimate.

The instruction was to maintain the handoff's content exactly wherever possible
rather than mixing interpretations. Each kit is therefore reproduced **verbatim**
as a *Canonical kit — 13 September 2026* block at the head of its class section
in `classes.md`, ahead of the developed material, and is the current statement
wherever the two differ. Existing developed detail — world dependencies, open
questions, Worksite interaction, branch rationale — is retained below each kit
rather than discarded, since the handoff does not address it.

## Material changes

**Daredevil** moves from substantially incomplete draft to a full kit
(`classes.md` §17, manuscript §9.15). The handoff supplies the previously
missing Ability 2 (Crash Landing, with Crater / Combat Roll / Superhero) and the
ultimate (Deathly Clutches). Superseded by it: the draft's Runway branches
Cannon Jump / Trampoline / Mach Headbutt, the older Skydiver wording specifying
one second airborne and extreme Speed, and the tentative Extraction archetype.
Waxer is now the only remaining draft. [OPEN] Whether Daredevil's kit equals a
completed conceptual pass of the kind Skeleton Crew and Lightfooted received is
the owner's call and is not asserted.

**Golem Master's passive is named Pumpkin Supply**, replacing "[Unnamed]".

**Kitfighter's ultimate is restated**: Covered With Diamonds projects
Diamond-level protection wherever current equipment is weaker, rather than
upgrading armor and tools wholesale, and carries movement speed and knockback
resistance. The handoff's own open item — interaction with equipment already
exceeding the projected Diamond state — is recorded as `[OPEN]`.

**Mole, Skeleton Crew and Lightfooted** kits agree with the existing settled
material; the verbatim blocks were added without changing the developed text.

## Preserved rather than reconciled

**Gardener's archetypes.** The kit reads "Development · Control · Support".
Control and Support are not members of the Seven Archetypes (Combat,
Construction, Development, Exploration, Extraction, Logistics, Production). The
line is reproduced exactly and flagged `[CONFLICT]` in `classes.md` §5, the
roster §11, and manuscript §9.4. It was neither mapped onto existing archetypes
nor quietly dropped. The roster still records Gardener's secondaries as
deliberately unassigned. Whether these are new archetypes, descriptive role
language, or replacements for the unassigned secondaries is the owner's
decision.

**Golem Master's audit flag.** The handoff states that Golem Master is the least
settled of the seven, that the base Assemble rule, generic material-stat rules
and Wither Golem's behaviour need one final conceptual pass, and that its exact
wording should not be silently promoted to canon. That instruction is carried
into the document with the kit. Its third archetype, Production, is recorded but
not adopted in the roster for the same reason.

## Not addressed by the handoff

Merchant (`classes.md` §13) and Waxer (§14) receive no kit and are unchanged.
The handoff describes seven classes; the documented roster is nine.

## Numbers

None supplied and none invented. Every quantity in the kits — durations, radii,
N in Deathly Clutches, Salvage thresholds, stun and velocity thresholds — remains
`[OPEN]` where the existing sections already said so, and is newly marked
`[OPEN]` for Daredevil.

# Second handoff — [K9] Merchant and Waxer

## Source

**[K9]** kit handoff covering the two classes [K7] did not address. Same
treatment: each kit is reproduced **verbatim** as a canonical kit block —
Merchant at the head of `classes.md` §13, Waxer as the new §18 — ahead of the
developed material and authoritative wherever the two differ.

## Merchant

The kit agrees with the developed active-design model: Work, Payroll, the
Novice→Master Mastery ladder, Swindle once per restock cycle with Invisible
Hand / Credit Line / Community Chest, Retinue with Seize the Means / Toll
Patrol / Overtime, and Procession scaling frequency by Retinue size and quality
by Mastery. Nothing in the developed section contradicts it, so nothing was
rewritten.

Merchant's status is **unchanged: active design, not settled.** The kit repeats
"Production (others pending final classification)" and lists its own open items —
Credit Line, Community Chest, Overtime, Emerald-substitution placement, the
final ultimate name and effect package, and final archetype classification. The
developed section's [FAINT CAUTION] markers on those same mechanics are
consistent with the kit and were left in place.

## Waxer

Waxer moves out of draft status (`classes.md` §18, manuscript §9.17). The kit
supplies the Ability 2 branch tree, previously recorded as "branch seeds not
equally developed", and the ultimate's delivery, previously "delivery mechanism
unresolved". Superseded: the draft's A1 branch sketches and the undeveloped A2
seeds. The draft's core rule survives as the hook.

Reproduced as supplied rather than edited: **Amber names both an A1 branch and
the ultimate.** This is a collision in the source, not a transcription error,
and renaming either would be an interpretation.

## Consequence for the roster

With [K7] and [K9] together, every documented class has a kit and **no class
remains an unsettled draft.** `classes.md` §14 and manuscript §9.12 were
converted from "Unsettled Class Drafts" into a **draft record** that preserves
how the roster reached that state rather than being deleted.

This is a coverage statement only. It does not assert that Daredevil and Waxer
received conceptual passes equivalent to Skeleton Crew's and Lightfooted's,
which were declared settled with explicit statements of what was decided; that
judgement is recorded as `[OPEN]` for the owner. Merchant is still explicitly
unsettled, Golem Master still carries its audit flag, and Gardener's archetype
line is still a recorded `[CONFLICT]`.

## Feasibility flagged, not assessed

Waxer's kit assumes Sealed blocks, per-block Wax accumulation, and durability
wear modified for players standing inside an area effect. None of these is
demonstrated in a datapack. The Waxer section records this as unassessed and
points at the capability audit rather than assuming the mechanics are reachable.

## Numbers

None supplied, none invented.
