# Reconciliation — Capacity, committed labour, and Skeleton Crew

Date: 2026-09-11 (handoff dated "late September 2026")

## Source

Logistics / Supply Line / Skeleton Crew Handoff `[WL]`, sections 1–17.

## Superseded

**1. Flow Weight → Capacity.** Capacity is how much cargo a logistical method
moves in one delivery, measured in **Minecraft inventory slots** at the carried
item's ordinary stack size. 64 cobblestone, 64 arrows, 16 ender pearls, one
pickaxe and one potion are each 1 Capacity.

This directly reverses the statement in `infrastructure.md` that the quantity is
*"not derived solely from literal Minecraft inventory slot count"* and functions
as a broader authored carrying-efficiency property. That framing was set by
`[WR]` only days earlier and is now `[HISTORICAL]`.

Capacity × Item Rate = throughput is unchanged. Item Rate still emerges from
real movement performance; the player authors neither number.

**2. Post-recognition freedom.** The previous framing — infrastructure
reproduces the flow without the original carrier, *"freeing the player or entity
to establish new value"* — is superseded as **too permissive**. It allowed a
worker to establish a line, leave to fight, and have the line continue as though
that worker were still labouring. Capacity committed to a Supply Line cannot
simultaneously be active in the world.

**3. Skeleton Crew A1 banking.** The banking-and-Arise formulation and the
banking of individual undead are superseded by Graveyard Shift and by abstract
Crew stacks generated from killing attracted hostile undead.

## Added

Committed Capacity and withdrawal; Item Rate unaffected by withdrawal; relay
node chains with a maximum supported span and progression on that span;
contestability layers; default summon behaviour (empty-handed follows; cargo
seeks nearest valid node); the general rule that Logistics classes stay involved
after establishment; Skeleton Crew's shared labour pool, sunlight vulnerability,
revised passive, Graveyard Shift, and Labored Union.

## Note on Summoning Capacity

`[WL]` §3 asks that a separate universal Summoning Capacity not be used to
constrain Skeleton Crew. **A global search found zero occurrences** of
"Summoning Capacity" anywhere in the repository, so nothing was removed. The
scoped decision not to introduce one — and the caution that Golem Master and
other non-Logistics summoners be reconsidered independently — is recorded.

## Left [OPEN]

records of what was decided at the time, which is the historical trace this
handoff asks to preserve. Retargeting them would destroy that trace. Remaining
"Flow Weight" occurrences outside those records are `[HISTORICAL]` markers that
explain the supersession, as instructed.
