# Reconciliation — phase-conditioned Task and archetype progression

## Authority and scope

This record integrates the 25 September 2026 progression discussion into
`classes.md`, which remains the canonical owner of class, archetype, and Task
progression. `objectives.md`, `infrastructure.md`, and `maps.md` remain owners
of their respective systems. No playable implementation or numerical balance
is implied.

## Integrated Working direction

Player progression, accumulated world development, and objective state are
interlocking curves. Early levels establish capability; middle levels organize
and exploit established value; late levels connect, contest, defend, repair,
or dismantle mature systems; endgame levels convert accumulated advantage into
decisive pressure.

The level ranges in `classes.md` are ecological assumptions, not time gates.
Objective state can move a match into a later strategic condition early, and
teams may have different levels of world development at the same player level.

Late Task specialization is conditional and increasingly powerful rather than
merely numerically larger:

> later specialization = greater magnitude × narrower ideal condition

This explicitly permits decisive late combat power for Slaying/Damage
investment. Balance comes from opportunity cost and from giving Yield,
Efficiency, and the seven archetypes equivalent opportunities to become
decisive in their own ideal situations.

Task investment compounds with class investment. Task investment describes the
depth of commitment to a Minecraft verb; class development describes the
methods and situations that make that verb matter. Late power consequently has
personal, accumulated-world, and interaction components.

## Models rejected or superseded by this reconciliation

1. **Linear tier magnitude:** every tier as a larger coefficient or capacity
   is rejected as the universal progression grammar. It does not account for
   the changed world state or objective state.
2. **Late combat restraint as a goal:** rejected. A committed combat specialist
   must be able to swing a decisive late fight. The requirement is parity of
   strategic decisiveness, not suppression of combat power.
3. **Rigid level/objective synchronization:** rejected. Levels and objective
   events should interact without making one a clock-gated prerequisite for the
   other.
4. **Late infrastructure replacing personal specialization:** rejected.
   Infrastructure and accumulated world investment amplify personal/class
   specialization; they do not make it unnecessary.

## Preserved existing decisions

- Archetypes remain descriptive, not generative.
- Class-authored eligibility still controls infrastructure access.
- The former universal Yield I / Efficiency I onboarding and sparse later Task
  cadence are superseded as the preferred model by recurring open Task choices
  at Levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, and 28. The old structure remains in git
  history for traceability; exact branch depth and numerical effects remain
  Open.
- Exact mechanics, coefficients, XP values, objective thresholds, world-state
  measurements, and implementation feasibility remain **Open**.

## Class-curve decision

The preferred template has three clocks: Development every three levels, Task
choice every four levels, and a compact ability track of engine at 0, Actives at
1–2, ability branches at 5 and 10, and a complete Ultimate at 15. The permanent
ability-by-five cadence is rejected as unnecessary kit mutation. Later levels
should primarily develop the class's method and persistent world footprint.

Development changes semantic role over time: establish the person and engine,
then establish the methodology, then scale the player's infrastructure,
capacity, connectivity, throughput, influence, simultaneous sites, and
sophistication. Personal late power remains possible, but the preferred late
scaling budget is increasingly team-facing and persistent.

Mole is the first qualitative application: Read → Exploit → Extend → Connect →
Integrate → Mature → Constraint-break → Complexity → Integration → Complete.
Its kit is treated as complete at Level 15; later development expands what the
Mole has made possible across the map rather than repeatedly mutating Sinkhole
or adding generic excavation multipliers.

## Additional rejected models

- **Universal Task onboarding followed by sparse specialization:** rejected in
  favour of offering Yield, Efficiency, and Slaying at every Task opportunity;
  the player may unlock or deepen any branch each time.
- **Permanent Ability ×5 cadence:** rejected as a default. It encourages
  needless late kit mutation and makes a readable ability feel incomplete.
- **Late supernormality as mostly individual inflation:** rejected as the
  preferred model. Personal supernormality is not forbidden, but the largest
  late-game expansion should usually be the class's persistent world footprint.
