# Primary Materials, Efficiency, and Offhander Exception — 14 September 2026

Status: **current-facing reconciliation**. This note corrects stale passages in `classes.md` and the 14 September economic supplement. Where those passages conflict with this file, this file supersedes them until the root documents are regenerated.

## Primary Materials

The current Primary Material category is:

- Copper
- Iron
- Gold
- Diamond
- Netherite
- Leather

Copper is therefore a Primary Material. The older `classes.md` passage that lists Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, and Leather while explicitly excluding Copper is superseded.

The category remains deliberately restricted: membership represents materials whose expenditure can directly represent significant portable player capability. Adding Copper does not imply that Redstone, Lapis, Coal, Emerald, Quartz, Flint, String, Wood, or Stone should also enter the category.

Kitfighter Salvage therefore recognizes Copper as a Primary Material whenever the crafted equipment otherwise satisfies Salvage's recipe requirement.

## Efficiency task family

The universal **Efficiency task family is Efficiency + Unbreaking**, not Efficiency alone.

Current cadence:

- Level 4 universal: **Efficiency I + Unbreaking I**.
- If the player advances Efficiency at Level 9: **Efficiency II + Unbreaking II**.
- If advanced again at Level 14: **Efficiency III + Unbreaking III**.
- Generic task-family ceiling remains Tier III unless class-specific progression explicitly exceeds it.

This pairing deliberately combines two different forms of work efficiency:

- **Efficiency enchantment** increases execution speed for eligible work.
- **Unbreaking** increases equipment persistence and reduces replacement/material/logistical burden.

Neither should be modeled as multiplying physical resource opportunity. Yield remains the task family that changes realized material per eligible opportunity.

Any current-facing `classes.md` wording defining Efficiency only as the Efficiency enchantment family is superseded by this pairing.

## Global offhand rule and Kitfighter exception

The global match rule remains:

> Ordinary players do not have a functional offhand slot.

This is intentional inventory/equipment pressure: shields, buckets, food, light, utility, tools, blocks, resources, and other mission equipment ordinarily compete for normal inventory and active-item handling rather than receiving a free secondary slot.

**Kitfighter's Ability 1 — Offhander deliberately supersedes that global rule.** The exception is the point of the ability and is consistent with its title and class fantasy.

Therefore the current Kitfighter wording remains valid:

- base Offhander equips a Bow in Kitfighter's otherwise unavailable offhand;
- Crossbow replaces the Bow with a Crossbow;
- Fishing Rod replaces it with a Fishing Rod;
- Golden Head replaces it with a Golden Head.

Offhander should be understood as a **class-authored exceptional capability**, not evidence that the global offhand slot exists. It temporarily/conditionally restores access to that equipment channel only through Kitfighter's authored ability.

The 14 September economic supplement statement that Offhander is incompatible with the global rule and requires redesign is superseded and should not be used in future reconciliation.

## Resulting design relationship

The intended hierarchy is:

`global rule: no functional offhand`

`+ Kitfighter authored exception: Offhander`

`= Kitfighter gains exceptional simultaneous equipment access that ordinary players do not possess`

This follows the project's broader class doctrine: ordinary Minecraft/system rules define the common player, while classes may author explicit exceptional interactions with those rules.
