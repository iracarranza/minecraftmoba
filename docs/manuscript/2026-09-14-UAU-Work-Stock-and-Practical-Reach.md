# UAU, Work Stock, Practical Reach, and Sawtooth Progression

**Design manuscript supplement • 14 September 2026**

This supplement records the current progression-accounting model developed after the 10 September manuscript snapshot. It should be read with Chapter 5, especially §§5.1–5.7. It does not finalize individual Work Point awards or absolute XP requirements.

## 1. Useful Action Units

A **Work Point (WP)** is the fine-grained accounting unit for progression-worthy work. A **Useful Action Unit (UAU)** is a standardized quantity of progression-worthy work used to compare heterogeneous useful activities. UAU is a unit of account, not an event: small legitimate actions may generate fractions of a UAU, while major accomplishments may generate one or multiple UAUs.

The current working calibration resolution is:

> **256 WP = 1 UAU.**

This resolution is a calibration hypothesis, not final balance canon. It is intentionally fine enough for small actions and for infrastructure progression multipliers around 1.10–1.15× to remain meaningful without requiring every action to award a large number.

Infrastructure multipliers should apply to accumulated progression credit rather than round each primitive action independently. Infrastructure does not retroactively increase how much physical work occurred; it increases the progression efficiency of otherwise legitimate work.

## 2. Repetition and anti-grind doctrine

Legitimate repeated work remains legitimate progression work. Ten thousand legitimately placed blocks should still generate progression credit because they represent real acquisition, movement, inventory, placement, and player-time. Do not use arbitrary diminishing returns, first-clear-only rules, or level-based invalidation merely to prevent grinding.

Instead, anti-grind should emerge from two curves:

1. progression requirements increase between economic bands; and
2. rational play gains access to increasingly productive opportunities as character capacity, Work Stock, world knowledge, infrastructure, and the opportunity frontier develop.

A primitive activity may therefore continue producing approximately constant UAU/min while becoming progressively less competitive at purchasing later levels. The work remains real; the economy has moved on.

## 3. UAU Flow, Work Stock, Opportunity Frontier, and Practical Reach

**UAU Flow** is the rate at which useful work is currently realized:

> **F = UAU / player-minute.**

**Work Stock** is persistent state created through earlier activity that changes what future work is practical. It is not synonymous with inventory wealth and is not itself UAU. It includes material stock, capital equipment, structural state, biological state, logistical positioning, information, and recognized networks.

Representative forms include:

- material stock: Copper, Iron, food, wood and other acquired inputs;
- capital stock: tools, armor and equipment;
- structural stock: bridges, tunnels, buildings and prepared Construction Blocks;
- biological stock: farms, animals and founder populations;
- logistical stock: caches, staged resources and supply organization;
- information stock: known terrain, deposits, POIs, Handoffs and routes of access;
- network stock: recognized infrastructure and its relationships.

The **Opportunity Frontier** is the set of useful work currently practical given character capability, Work Stock, world state, knowledge, geography, and current threats/opportunities.

**Practical Reach** describes whether a particular undertaking is economically realizable, not merely physically possible. Relevant evidence includes capacity, resources, access, endurance, equipment, knowledge, support, threat, acquisition burden, economic breadth, cargo demand, processing, return burden, exposure, and coordination. These dimensions are evidence, not necessarily terms in one additive numerical formula.

The core progression loop is:

```text
USEFUL WORK
→ UAU Flow
→ character progression + persistent Work Stock
→ greater Practical Reach
→ larger Opportunity Frontier
→ higher-value / higher-throughput useful work
→ greater UAU Flow
```

## 4. Levels generally alter practicality, not permission

Ordinary Minecraft work should not normally be forbidden because a player has not reached a level. A low-level player may build a large structure, reach a deep cave, find Gold, or prepare a distant farm if actual Minecraft conditions permit it. Early constraints instead arise from small inventory, limited Hunger and food stock, primitive tools, resource breadth, processing, transport, threat, and lack of accumulated world knowledge.

A large glass-windowed remote structure illustrates the distinction. A low-level player may be physically able to build it, but doing so may require bulk blocks, sand, fuel, smelting, Iron for a bucket, food, tools, repeated trips, and heterogeneous inventory. A smaller structure may therefore be rational early while the larger project becomes routine only after Work Stock and capacity mature.

Exploration follows the same principle. A deep location is not inherently level-gated. Its practical requirements may include outbound and return endurance, food, light, tools, blocks, cargo capacity, terrain solutions, threat tolerance, and knowledge. Early players can sometimes reach it, but doing so may consume nearly their entire loadout and leave little capacity to exploit or return with value.

## 5. Level 6 as Recognition Breakpoint

Level 6 should be understood as an **authored Recognition Breakpoint inside an organic economic transition**, not as the moment Construction or infrastructure-shaped physical work becomes possible.

Before Level 6, players may already build structures and paths, establish farms, move and cache resources, explore distant terrain, prepare Construction Blocks, and organize productive sites. Their limits are principally material and logistical.

At Level 6, selected qualifying prior state can receive first formal infrastructure recognition. Earlier work can therefore become a basis for greater throughput without being retroactively treated as nonexistent before recognition.

This creates two overlapping transitions:

- **organic transition:** character growth and accumulated Work Stock expand Practical Reach;
- **authored transition:** Level 6 permits recognition of selected already-created state.

The Level 7 requirement jump should not be justified by infrastructure alone. It is a hypothesis about the combined productivity increase produced by capacity growth, accumulated stock, better equipment, broader opportunities, task progression, first infrastructure, and integration effects.

## 6. Sawtoothed XP bands

The existing working requirement indices remain:

| Band | Levels | Relative requirement index |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bootstrap | 1–6 | ~1.000× |
| Established | 7–12 | ~1.350× |
| Developed | 13–19 | ~1.875× |
| Advanced | 20–24 | ~2.575× |
| Endgame | 25–30 | ~3.250× |

The transitions therefore increase requirements by approximately +35.0%, +38.9%, +37.3%, and +26.2% respectively.

These are not arbitrary escalating grind bands. Each transition hypothesizes a corresponding expansion in the amount or productivity of useful work that rational play can realize. Within a band, capability and Work Stock may improve while the requirement scale remains comparatively stable, causing progression efficiency to improve. At the next band, the requirement rises and the player must increasingly exploit the opportunity frontier made practical by earlier development.

For a primitive activity with roughly constant UAU productivity, its relative effectiveness against the requirement indices falls to about 100%, 74%, 53%, 39%, and 31% from Bootstrap through Endgame. The action has not been nerfed; later progression expects a more developed economy.

The correct validation target for a transition is therefore the ratio between rational UAU productivity in adjacent economic phases, not the size of one unlock or one XP multiplier.

## 7. Work Stock and archetypes

Different avenues characteristically create different forms of persistent stock or opportunity:

| Avenue | Characteristic persistent result |
|---|---|
| Exploration | knowledge, Handoffs, access and geographic understanding |
| Extraction | acquired material stock and exposed resource access |
| Construction | useful geometry and structural capital |
| Production | processed goods and productive/player capability |
| Development | mature renewable populations and productive world state |
| Logistics | positioned stock and delivery relationships |
| Combat | secured or denied opportunity windows and control state |

Combat is comparatively flow-oriented: it often creates a temporary window in which another player can realize value. This does not require generic XP sharing. A single economic chain can generate separate progression credit for separate useful labor: Exploration for resolving access, Construction for creating geometry, Extraction for acquisition, Combat for securing value under threat, Logistics for making resources available, and Production for transformation.

## 8. Material taxonomy and economic maturity

The existing **Primary Material** category should remain a mechanical tag where systems such as Kitfighter Salvage need it. It should not be forced to represent the entire economic progression ladder.

Current Primary Materials remain Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, and Leather unless separately revised.

For economic analysis, distinguish overlapping roles such as:

- **bulk resources** — ordinary high-volume material;
- **capital materials** — inputs substantially invested into persistent productive/player capability;
- **system inputs** — materials such as Lapis, Redstone, Gold in many uses, and fuel that enable broader systems;
- **structural capital** — prepared Construction Blocks;
- **consumptive inputs** — food, fuel and other operationally consumed resources;
- **founder/biological stock** — crops, breeding animals and renewable populations.

A material may occupy multiple roles.

Separately, economic phase describes when a resource relationship is normally practical, not when the resource is allowed to exist or be acquired. Current analytical hypothesis:

| Economy | Characteristic material relationships |
|---|---|
| Bootstrap | Wood, Stone, Coal, Copper, basic food and bulk construction |
| Established | Copper maturity, Iron, prepared structural capital |
| Developed | Iron maturity, Lapis, Redstone, Gold and stronger system inputs |
| Advanced | Gold/Diamond and advanced Production/enchanting relationships |
| Endgame | Diamond maturity, Ancient Debris/Netherite conversion and apex inputs |

Exceptional geography or play can get ahead of this profile; poorly prepared players can fall behind it.

## 9. Construction Blocks as structural capital

Construction Blocks should be analyzed as **structural capital**, not merely as blocks carrying an XP premium. They represent deliberate conversion of resources into stored Construction investment. Ordinary blocks remain cheap immediate geometry. Construction Blocks are prepared material investment capable of greater progression relevance and, when incorporated into recognized Constructs, Structural Integrity and infrastructure value.

The conceptual chain is:

```text
resource acquisition
→ preparation/transformation
→ stored structural investment
→ placement
→ Construction progression contribution
→ Construct incorporation
→ Structural Integrity / recognized infrastructure
```

This framing does not yet change the exact Construction Block recipes, eligible materials, WP premium, or recognition rules.

## 10. UAU attribution and material output

XP should measure completed useful work rather than commodity wealth. Resource stock changes the opportunity frontier but does not automatically grant UAU merely by existing in a chest.

For Extraction, preserve the distinction:

> **WHAT YOU GOT → material economy.**
>
> **WHAT IT TOOK TO GET IT → Extraction progression.**

Resource-Intrinsic Acquisition Profile plus worldgen Opportunity/Commitment evidence should classify acquisition significance. Yield/Fortune increases material obtained from an opportunity rather than multiplying the underlying acquisition UAU. Efficiency increases the rate at which work can be completed. Unbreaking lowers the equipment cost of sustaining that work.

Thus:

- Yield increases **material / UAU**;
- Efficiency increases **UAU / time**;
- Unbreaking decreases **equipment cost / UAU**.

## 11. Calibration protocol

Absolute XP requirements should be calibrated from undertaking-level simulations rather than invented per-resource XP prices.

For an undertaking, track acquisition, breadth, cargo, access, endurance, exploitation, processing, return, exposure, knowledge, and coordination burdens. Estimate total player-time across prerequisite acquisition, travel, processing, execution, return, and overhead. Track WP generated across every relevant avenue and the persistent Work Stock left behind.

Evaluate the same undertaking at representative player states such as Lv1, Lv3, Lv5, Lv6 without recognition, and Lv6 with recognition. Key comparisons are:

1. **character effect** — same stock, later character state;
2. **stock effect** — same character state, opening versus realistically accumulated stock;
3. **recognition effect** — same physical state with and without formal infrastructure recognition;
4. **full phase effect** — rational Established UAU productivity divided by rational Bootstrap UAU productivity.

Only the fourth comparison should be tested directly against the current ~1.35 Band-II requirement index.

Construction is the first numerical reference archetype because physical placements, material movement, processing, and recognized completion can be measured relatively directly. Initial sensitivity fixtures may use ordinary useful placement = 1 WP, Construction Block placement = 2 WP total, and baseline Construct recognition = +64 WP, but none of those individual awards are canon until undertaking throughput is tested.

## 12. Current doctrine

> **Useful Action Units measure progression-worthy work rather than resource wealth, output market value, or designer-approved play patterns. The working accounting resolution is 256 Work Points per UAU. Small legitimate actions may generate individual Work Points, while strategically significant outcomes generate larger amounts. Repetition does not inherently diminish or invalidate work. Instead, increasingly expensive progression bands assume that character growth, accumulated Work Stock, expanding Practical Reach, and new system recognition expose increasingly productive work. Primitive activity therefore remains valid but becomes competitively insufficient as the economy matures.**
>
> **Work Stock is any persistent material, structural, biological, logistical, informational, capital, or network state created through play that reduces the cost of or expands access to future useful work. Levels generally do not grant permission to perform ordinary Minecraft work; they alter player capacity and, at selected breakpoints such as Level 6, permit formal recognition of already-created state. Economic resource phases likewise describe when opportunities are normally practical, not when resources are allowed to exist or be acquired.**
>
> **The sawtoothed XP bands should ultimately be calibrated against measured changes in rational UAU productivity across economic phases. Their current ~1.00 / 1.35 / 1.875 / 2.575 / 3.25 indices are hypotheses about the productive expansion of the match economy, not arbitrary escalating grind requirements.**
