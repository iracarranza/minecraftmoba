Status

Working design. This replaces the earlier candidate objective-family model (Stronghold / Excavation / Monument / Expedition / Siege) with a concrete three-layer objective structure.

Minecraft already supplies many avenues of progression. Objectives should concentrate and contest Minecraft play, not replace the sandbox with a collection of bespoke minigames.
1 · Objective Structure

Three objective layers

  1. Four team objectives — persistent structures taking the place of MOBA turrets/base defenses.
  2. Dedicated non-combat objectives — “jungle camp”-scale opportunities centered on construction, extraction, logistics, development, and related Minecraft verbs.
  3. Major neutral objectives — combat encounters whose defeat directly damages the enemy's corresponding team objective and grants massive XP.

This deliberately does not require one objective for every avenue of play.

2 · Team Objectives

Four persistent team structures

  1. The Pillager Outpost
  2. The Nether Bastion
  3. The End Tower
  4. The Aether Fountain

The first three support multiple defeat conditions, with a working XP ordering:

Structural destruction < Defender elimination < Objective-specific interaction

Brute force remains valid; the specialized solution rewards interacting with the structure's Minecraft identity.

2.1 · The Pillager Outpost

The earliest and most accessible defensive objective.

  • Destruction — lowest XP. Destroy enough of the structure for the Outpost to topple.
  • Elimination — medium XP. Kill all defending Pillagers.
  • Allay Theft — highest XP. Steal the Allay that powers the Outpost.

The Allay route creates an infiltration/extraction problem rather than another health bar.

Flavor: Overworld / occupation / raiding / theft.

2.2 · The Nether Bastion

A tougher defensive layer.

  • Destruction — lowest XP. Destroy enough of the Bastion. Its construction makes this substantially harder than destroying the Outpost.
  • Elimination — medium XP. Kill all defending Piglins.
  • Gold-Hoard Extraction — highest XP. Reach the center and extract the Bastion's gold hoard.

This makes the specialized solution an extraction problem: penetrate, secure, and remove the hoard rather than demolishing the whole fortification.

Flavor: Nether / fortification / greed / extraction.

2.3 · The End Tower

The final defensive layer before the Fountain.

  • Destruction — lowest XP. Destroy enough of the tower. Its extensive Obsidian construction makes brute-force destruction possible but deliberately difficult.
  • Elimination — medium XP. Kill all defending Endermen.
  • End Crystal Destruction — highest XP. Reach the top and destroy the objective's End Crystal. Unlike a vanilla crystal, it is substantially more durable and has a visible HP bar.

This creates a vertical assault problem.

Flavor: End / verticality / extreme durability / assault.

3 · Final Team Objective

The Aether Fountain

The Aether Fountain is the final team objective.

It begins protected and becomes exposed as defensive objectives fall. The exact exposure rule remains unresolved.

The Fountain is a functioning Minecraft structure rather than primarily another combat health bar.

It can be disabled by:

  • Source obstruction: obstruct its source block so the Fountain ceases to function.
  • Total destruction: destroy enough of the Fountain to stop its operation.

Respawning

If the Aether Fountain stops functioning, that team's players can no longer respawn.
breach defenses → disable Fountain → subsequent deaths become permanent → eliminate surviving defenders

Whether a disabled Fountain can be repaired/reactivated is unresolved.

4 · Multiple Solutions and XP

Completion methods are recognized, not preselected

MethodCharacterXP
Sufficient structural destructionBrute force / extraction / siegeLowest
Kill all defendersCombat clearanceMedium
Objective-specific interactionInfiltration / extraction / specialized playHighest

Destruction is not an incorrect solution. It consumes tools, time, positioning, and often materials. It is the universal Minecraft fallback.

The objective recognizes the completion condition, not a route declared in advance. Mixed attacks are expected: players might kill Pillagers while tunneling toward the Allay, demolish a Bastion wall to reach its gold, or build upward while fighting Endermen to reach the crystal.

Exact XP values and distribution are unresolved.

5 · Dedicated Non-Combat Objectives

Jungle-camp scale Minecraft work

Smaller neutral objectives replace the earlier broad candidate-family list.

These are analogous in strategic scale to jungle camps: optional, valuable opportunities that create reasons to leave established territory and perform Minecraft-native work. Enemy players can contest them, but combat is not their primary interaction.

5.1 · Mining Outpost

Players construct a defensible, functional structure at a designated site.

Successful completion temporarily exposes or grants access to rare ores in the surrounding area.

The objective should use the ordinary Construction evaluation system rather than a bespoke building minigame. Candidate validation includes:

  • sufficient committed construction;
  • protected/enclosed usable area;
  • access;
  • functional blocks;
  • defensibility/obstruction;
  • minimum project scale.

Center: Construction
Strong overlap: Extraction, Logistics
Contest pressure: Combat, Exploration

The reward is physical and Minecraft-native: building successfully changes the world into a valuable extraction site.

5.2 · Supply Chain

A valuable development zone appears in terrain deliberately difficult to access or supply.

Candidate environments:

  • waterlocked islands;
  • floating terrain;
  • lava pockets;
  • isolated cliffs;
  • other disconnected regions.

The zone contains unique crops and development resources.

Completion requires establishing a usable supply route between accessible team territory/infrastructure and the target zone. Evaluation should focus on actual connectivity/traversal rather than prescribing a number of rails or blocks.

Valid solutions might include bridges, boats, railways, tunnels, vertical transport, or combinations.

Center: Logistics
Strong overlap: Construction, Exploration, Development
Possible overlap: Extraction

6 · Major Neutral Objectives

Combat-focused boss sequence

  1. Giant Zombie
  2. Ghast
  3. Ender Dragon

Defeating one grants:

  1. Massive XP
  2. Direct damage to the corresponding enemy team objective
Neutral objectiveEnemy objective damaged
Giant ZombiePillager Outpost
GhastNether Bastion
Ender DragonEnd Tower
Strategic alternative: attack the enemy structure directly or contest and defeat the neutral monster to damage it indirectly.

The exact form and amount of objective damage are unresolved. It should materially change the corresponding siege without making direct interaction with the objective's three defeat routes irrelevant.

7 · Match Escalation and Flavor

Overworld → Nether → End → Aether

  • Early — Pillager / Giant Zombie: accessible materials, conventional enemies, theft/infiltration.
  • Mid — Nether / Ghast: tougher fortification, environmental complications, gold extraction, ranged threat.
  • Late — End / Ender Dragon: Obsidian durability, vertical assault, durable End Crystal, strongest neutral combat encounter.
  • Final — Aether Fountain: the contest shifts from destroying another defensive layer to disabling the enemy team's ability to respawn.
Overworld → Nether → End → Aether

This does not require literal vanilla dimensional progression.

8 · Relationship to Ordinary Progression

Objectives are not the sole source of progression

Players still gain resources and XP through recognized Minecraft accomplishments such as:

  • extracting ore deposits;
  • establishing productive farms;
  • enclosing/developing animals;
  • completing submitted construction projects;
  • meaningful exploration/discovery;
  • establishing useful transport infrastructure;
  • production;
  • combat.
Objectives answer: “Where is unusually valuable work available right now?”

Not: “What is the only thing worth doing right now?”

Conceding an objective to pursue mining, development, construction, exploration, an enemy assault, or another opportunity can therefore be rational.

9 · Design Analysis

Strengths

  • Minecraft-native objective interaction. Team objectives use ordinary world verbs: break, kill, steal, extract, climb, obstruct.
  • Distinct solutions without class locks. No objective says “bring the Builder.” Different avenues can create different routes to the same strategic result.
  • Non-combat objective-scale play. Mining Outpost and Supply Chain concentrate construction/logistics/development value without requiring a dedicated objective for every archetype.
  • Clear combat objective home. The Giant Zombie → Ghast → Ender Dragon sequence creates major combat contests and massive XP without turning every smaller neutral objective into a monster camp.
  • Multiple simultaneous priorities. Enemy siege, neutral boss, non-combat objective, resource acquisition, infrastructure, development, and harassment can coexist.

Key Risks / Unresolved Questions

Objective sequence

Is Pillager → Bastion → End Tower strictly sequential, are later layers merely protected until earlier ones fall, or can difficult Minecraft-native routes bypass layers?

Aether exposure

Does the Fountain become attackable after any one objective falls, become progressively vulnerable with each loss, or only fully expose after all three?

Aether obstruction

A single unnoticed block should probably not be equivalent to instantly destroying a Nexus. Source obstruction may need to persist for a validation/channel period, or defenders may be able to restore function while alive.

Structural destruction

“Sufficient destruction” needs a robust threshold. Pure percentage destruction risks teaching players to identify and remove the cheapest arbitrary blocks rather than meaningfully topple the structure.

Defenders

Pillagers, Piglins, and Endermen need enough encounter design that elimination is a genuine route rather than clearing static vanilla mobs.

Neutral boss damage

Too much damage makes the three-route enemy objectives irrelevant; too little makes bosses strategically secondary despite their intended importance.

XP economy

“Massive XP” and high-XP specialized captures must fit the 1–30 progression curve without allowing one early objective to skip most character development.

10 · Working Summary
Team structures give the enemy Minecraft-native siege problems.
Non-combat neutral objectives create concentrated opportunities to build, extract, transport, and develop.
Major neutral monsters create high-stakes combat contests and indirectly advance sieges.
Ordinary Minecraft remains a valid source of progression between and around all three.

This replaces the need for one objective corresponding to every avenue while preserving Minecraft's intentionally broad progression space.
