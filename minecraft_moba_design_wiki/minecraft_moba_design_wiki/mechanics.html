Core mechanic inventory

  • Destructible objectives: structures can fall through actual material destruction rather than only abstract HP.
  • Alternate objective verbs: killing defenders, stealing entities, extracting resources, and sabotaging blocks can all complete objectives.
  • Respawn infrastructure: the Aether Fountain physically represents a team's ability to return to play.
  • Add new mechanic: describe the rule, why it belongs in Minecraft, and what decisions it creates.

Mechanic test

For every new mechanic, answer:

  1. What does the player physically do?
  2. What information do opponents have?
  3. What is the counterplay?
  4. Why is this better than the conventional MOBA version?
  5. Does it create a Minecraft interaction, or merely look Minecraft-themed?
