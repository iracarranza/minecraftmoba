# Authored world, published for non-Minecraft readers

balanced_baseline is the block-exact world authored from the **pre-v2** frontier SHA-256 444594cd47198c08354d2829d8e96a2b97243e5c757aa3eb67b226d138699ab5. It remains valid evidence about proxy error and Route spillover, but it is not validation of newer v2 optimizer finalists.

## Published forms

- balanced_baseline-map.png: 432x528 sampled orthographic surface render, 4-block sampling, 2 px/sample.
- balanced_baseline-grid.json: the same 216x264 surface sample grid as run-length encoded data, plus every authored site and Route.

The grid preserves each Route's **full crossing records** (kind, detail, cell and world position) rather than only crossing kinds. Future publications generated by publish_world.py reject a grid unless every encoded sample round-trips exactly to the in-memory sampled world.

## Important semantics

routes[].weighted_cost is the **pre-carving terrain-graph cost used to select the corridor**. It is not final post-authoring Practical Reach. Use ../rescan/rescan-balanced_baseline.json for measured reach and balance.

routes[].columns is the densified Route centreline length in columns; the physical corridor is three blocks wide.

## Not covered

The published surface products do not show underground blocks, entities, or block-exact detail between 4-block samples.
